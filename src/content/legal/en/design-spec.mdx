A model that does not meet this specification counts as a customer stress test — see the disclaimer in the [After-Sales Policy](/legal/after-sales).

## 1. General Modelling Principles

- Build solid models with parametric software (SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Rhino, etc.)
- Do not export surface ("NURBS") models directly — convert them to closed solids first
- Check the model for self-intersections, overlapping faces, zero-thickness faces and other defects

## 2. Tolerances and Fits

- Clearance fit (sliding / rotating): leave 0.2–0.3 mm per side
- Interference fit: −0.05 to −0.1 mm per side (adjust for material shrinkage)

## 3. Overhangs and Supports

- Overhang length ≤ 4 × the thickness of the feature itself; beyond that, add ribs or external supports
- Bridge length: ≤ 50 mm recommended

## 4. Holes and Grooves

- Minimum through-hole diameter: ≥ 1.5 mm (≥ 2.0 mm recommended)
- Deep blind holes: depth-to-diameter ratio ≤ 4:1; beyond that, leave room for powder / liquid drainage

## 5. Text and Textures

- Raised text: height ≥ 0.5 mm, width ≥ 0.5 mm
- Engraved text: depth ≥ 0.5 mm, width ≥ 0.5 mm
- Fine textures need to be confirmed with us for process feasibility
