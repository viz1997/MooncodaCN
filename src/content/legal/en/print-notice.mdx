This notice applies to **print service work orders** — the orders you place by uploading a model and paying a weight-based quote. Ready-made products in the store do not follow this notice; see each product page instead.

## 1. File Requirements

- Make sure the model is a closed solid: no broken faces, flipped faces or non-manifold geometry
- Recommended export tolerances: chord deviation ≤ 0.05 mm, angular deviation ≤ 1°
- Keep a single file under 100 MB; contact us in advance for very large models

## 2. Size and Accuracy

- Maximum print size depends on the process; SLA goes up to 300 × 300 × 300 mm
- Industrial accuracy ±0.05 mm; for batch parts, the first article is the reference

## 3. Wall Thickness

- Minimum wall thickness ≥ 1.1 mm
- Overhangs beyond 45° need supports, or contact us to assess printability

## 4. Surface Finishing

- Parts ship in full colour as printed by default
- Post-processing may affect the lead time — the quote is authoritative

## 5. Payment and Shipping

- Production starts after the quote is confirmed and paid
- Pick your delivery window on the quote page: 96 hours (standard, no surcharge), 72 hours (express) or 48 hours (urgent). The express and urgent tiers are charged as a **percentage of the quote subtotal** — the price of each tier is shown in the quote breakdown before you order
- SF Express with cash on delivery by default; see the site for the free-shipping regions
- For international shipping, contact us for a dedicated quote
