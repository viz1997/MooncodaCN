Dear customer, it is **strictly prohibited to use the Mooncoda platform to order the production of prohibited items**, such as controlled knives, firearms or ammunition.

Once discovered, the account is closed immediately and the case is reported to the public security authorities. Please comply with the platform rules — thank you for your support and cooperation!

---

Before you submit a model, make sure it meets the requirements in the [Print Notice](/legal/print-notice) and the [3D Model Design Specification](/legal/design-spec).
