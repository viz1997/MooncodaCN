# Shipping & Delivery

This page covers two kinds of orders: **catalog products** (finished items, shipped after payment) and **print jobs** (you upload your own model; the studio quotes it by weight and then schedules production).

> Prices on this site are in **CNY (RMB)**. The amount on the checkout page or the job page is what actually gets charged.

## 1. Catalog products: shipping fee

| Case | Shipping |
|---|---|
| Discounted subtotal **at or above ¥99** | **Free** |
| Discounted subtotal **below ¥99** | **¥8** |

- The threshold is measured against the **discounted subtotal** (quantity tier discounts and coupon codes are applied first). A "free shipping" coupon sets the shipping fee to zero.
- The shipping fee is shown both in the cart and on the checkout page, and it matches what you are charged.
- **Print jobs do not have a shipping fee** — what they carry is a **delivery surcharge** instead (see section 2).

## 2. Print jobs: delivery tiers and surcharges

Print jobs come in three delivery tiers, chosen in the order dialog. The surcharge is a **percentage of the subtotal** (subtotal = weight fee + post-processing fee + support fee):

| Tier | Time | Surcharge |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 96 hours | none |
| Express | 72 hours | **10%** of subtotal |
| Urgent | 48 hours | **37%** of subtotal |

- The surcharge for each tier is shown in the quote breakdown **before** you place the order — what the page shows is what you pay.
- Delivery time is counted from the moment **payment completes**; scheduling and printing both happen within it.
- Express and urgent shorten the delivery time only; they do not change the printing process or the accuracy (see [Print Notice](/legal/print-notice)).

## 3. Receiving and inspection

- **Catalog products**: the shipping fee is paid at checkout (¥8, or free above the threshold); we ship by **SF Express**.
- **Print jobs**: there is **no shipping line** — what they carry is the **delivery surcharge** (see section 2), and SF Express collects on delivery.
- Transit time is whatever the carrier delivers; it is **not counted** in the delivery tier above.
- Please **keep the unboxing video** and check the appearance, quantity and specification **in front of the courier**.
- Report any quality issue within **3 days** of delivery, following the [After-Sales Policy](/legal/after-sales).

## 4. Ordering from outside mainland China

Orders outside mainland China should be placed through our **Shopify store** — use the announcement bar at the top of the page or the "Shopify" entry in the navigation. Shipping fees and delivery options for overseas orders are shown on that store's checkout page.

## 5. Payment methods

- **WeChat Pay** (QR code) and **Alipay** (desktop web payment). Both settle directly into the studio's merchant accounts.
- Print jobs are paid on the job detail page; catalog orders are paid on the order page.
