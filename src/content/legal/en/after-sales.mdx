# After-Sales Policy

This policy applies to print service work orders. For ready-made products in the store, follow the notes on each product page.

## 1. Recommendations to Protect Your Rights

1. Keep the unboxing video. Inspect the goods in front of the courier when you sign — check the appearance, the quantity and the specification. If anything is wrong, contact our support right away.
2. Avoid having someone else sign on your behalf, or accepting the delivery when the courier is not present.
3. As soon as you receive the goods, open the packaging and store the parts somewhere ventilated, dry, at a moderate temperature and away from direct light. Improper storage may cause damage.
4. If you find a quality problem within 3 days of delivery, contact our after-sales team.

## 2. Refunds and Replacements

1. If Mooncoda's printing causes missing, damaged or deformed parts, file an after-sales request within 3 days of receiving the goods. Once we confirm it is our fault, we process the refund or the reprint within 2 hours.
2. Coupons already used on an order cannot be returned.
3. Where a quality problem is our fault, we only cover the cost of the affected model itself and accept no other liability.

## 3. Disclaimers

1. Anything that does not meet the [3D Model Design Specification](/legal/design-spec) is treated as a customer stress test and is not eligible for return or exchange.
2. The following fall outside the scope of returns and exchanges:
   - a. moisture, discolouration or deformation caused by improper storage after delivery;
   - b. products that have been used;
   - c. assemblies designed without clearance, and therefore cannot be assembled;
   - d. parts whose deviation is within the general accuracy (for resin, general accuracy is within 0.2 MM or 0.3%);
   - e. parts with slight resin deformation;
   - f. anything else caused by the customer.
3. Complaints that are not raised within 3 days of receiving the goods are no longer accepted.
